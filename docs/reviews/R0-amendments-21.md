## A-138 (GD-26 findings 1-3, review question 3, BUG-2) 05 s6.3's R1 band rule is canonical for the autopilot Bomber's attack altitude; the Deck dive under a known Flak is the designed counterplay, not a bug; the 850-940 m first sight is the Deck horizon; A-128's "real `BomberBrain` at 600 m" means the brain with its band heights pinned by the scenario; the band JSONs live in `test/sim/balance/`

**Evidence.**

1. **02 and 05 agree on the brain; only the C-06 row reads otherwise.** 05 s6.3 R1: "ingress band: Deck (60 m AGL) if the target cell has Flak or Missile threat and no Autocannon; High (600 m) if Autocannon only; otherwise Low (250 m)". 02 s11.2 Bombing row restates it word for word ("Picks a band by the target cell's defence (Deck vs Flak/Missile, High vs Autocannon only, Low otherwise), releases level"). 02 s10.3 names the Flak's blind spot as the design ("Deck, 40-150 m AGL: Flak **no**"; "there is no safe altitude over a mixed defence. A base with Autocannons and Flak forces the attacker to choose which tower to face"), 02 s10.1 notes say "Dart vs Flak: a deck bomb run under 150 m is immune" and 02 s4.4's `flak_battery` row lists "flying Deck (< 150 m rel)" as its blind spot. `attack_run.gd` `_set_band` implements exactly this (`THREAT_GUN` absent and `THREAT_FLAK | THREAT_MISSILE` present -> `Band.DECK`), and SC-29 pins the same rule for the Dart. Four sections and the code say Deck; one scenario row says 600 m. There is no third position to invent: the brain is right and the C-06 row describes a *setup*, not the brain.
2. **What C-06 measures is still real.** 02 s10.4's worked rows ("One Flak Battery vs a lone Bomber at 600 m ... survives"; "Two ... dies before release") are the Flak-DPS-versus-Bomber-HP anchor check: 30 x 2 shells per 2 s at "~50 % hits" = 15 DPS over the 12.4 s a 70 m/s Bomber spends between the 1,500 m envelope edge and its release point. A Bomber *is* inside the Flak envelope in play whenever the defence is mixed (Autocannon + Flak -> Low, 250 m, inside `h_rel` 150-1,500), whenever the Flak is not yet seen by its team (Low by default until `_set_band` re-evaluates at an R2 think), and whenever a human flies it; the altitude the brain chooses when it *does* know is the counterplay, and the Flak's lethality once a Bomber is in its band is the balance question. 600 m stays because 02 s10.4 computes at 600 m and C-01 flies at 600 m: one anchor altitude, not two.
3. **The horizon is physics, not a precondition failure.** 02 s10.3: "Deck, 40-150 m AGL: horizon 690-1,340 m". One-sided `sqrt(2 R h + h^2)` at h = 60 m is 851 m; the 850-940 m GD-26 measured on the shipped bands is that number plus the Flak's own height term. A-128's ">= 1,500 m first seen" was written for a Bomber at 600 m (visible to 2.2-4.5 km, 02 s10.3) and holds there (GD-26: 1,588-1,600 m). Applied to a Deck Bomber it asserts the opposite of what 02 s10.3 promises. The precondition is therefore scoped to the pinned 600 m leg; on the shipped bands the control leg asserts the Deck behaviour instead (decision below).
4. **The rig's override is the setup A-128 meant.** `c06_rig.gd` sets `deck_agl`, `band_low` and `band_high` to `rel_m` (600) and otherwise runs the real `BomberBrain` ATTACK (R1 route, R2 level run, R3 bomb-sight release, R4 egress) against the real `TowerBrain`; the control with no Flak released 8 / 8; same-seed runs are byte-identical. The review's one root cause stands: the override must be *asserted* (height held while inside `range_m`) and the shipped-band behaviour must be *pinned*, so the suite fails when either the brain or the content moves, instead of silently measuring another scenario.
5. **The path.** `tools/schemas/sim_scenario.schema.json` has `additionalProperties: false` and `test_scenarios.gd` (TA-33) runs every file under `test/sim/scenarios/`; a band file there would need a tools change for no gain, and 02 s18.3's heading already says `godot/test/sim/balance/`. A-128's path was a slip.

**Decision (binding).**

- **Canonical altitude:** 05 s6.3 R1 (restated in 02 s11.2 and s10.3) sets the autopilot Bomber's attack altitude. A Bomber that knows a Flak or Missile Tower covers its target, and no Autocannon, flies the Deck band (`deck_alt_agl_m`, 60 m) under the Flak's `h_rel_min` 150. This is **counterplay, not a bug**; neither `deck_alt_agl_m` nor `flak_battery.h_rel_min` changes. 02 s18.3 C-06's "600 m" is a **scenario setup** that pins the brain's three `attack_run` band heights (`deck_alt_agl_m`, `band_low_agl_m`, `band_high_agl_m`) to 600 m, and the row says so. 05 s6.3 gains one sentence to that effect (patched here).
- **BUG-2 is closed as not-a-bug** and re-scoped (acceptance_replace below): no brain or def change; the sim_ai lane adds **SC-53 `bomber_deck_under_flak`** (05 s11.2 catalogue, appended here; the Bomber analogue of SC-29) in `test/sim/ai/`: a Bomber ATTACK on a structure covered by one visible Flak and no Autocannon, 10 seeds: `AttackRun` band DECK at R2, height over the Flak below `h_rel_min` while inside `range_m`, the Flak fires 0 shells, the stick is released; a variant with one Autocannon added picks LOW (250 m) and the Flak fires; `attack_run.gd`'s header and `sim_ai/brains/MODULE.md` cite A-138. OWNER: re-tier BUG-2 to Sonnet, 0.25 d (the patch keys cannot change tier; default: the owner re-tiers when approving).
- **A-128 preconditions, scoped:** "first seen >= 1,500 m" (and, per the review nit, <= `sensor_radius` plus one tick of travel) applies to the pinned 600 m leg. On the shipped bands the C-06 control leg (A-139) asserts the Deck ingress and records the first-seen distance; 850-940 m is the expected value, not a failure.
- **Band JSON path ratified:** `godot/test/sim/balance/c06_bomber_vs_flak.json` and `c08_dart_duel.json`. A-128's `test/sim/scenarios/` reads `test/sim/balance/` (erratum in `R0-amendments-19.md`, pointer on the register row). No tools task.

**Not accepted:** raising `deck_alt_agl_m` to 150 or lowering `flak_battery.h_rel_min` (removes the Flak's designed blind spot; contradicts 02 s4.4, s10.1, s10.3 and SC-29); a Bomber-only band exception in `AttackRun` (a brain rule no 02 / 05 text supports and a second altitude vocabulary); re-reading C-06 as a shipped-band test (it would measure the Flak's blind spot, which C-02 already pins, and never its lethality); a C-06 at Low (250 m) with an Autocannon added to force the band (confounds two towers' damage in one number).

## A-139 (GD-26 finding 1, A-128 fallback) C-06's setup: the real `BomberBrain` with its band heights pinned to 600 m, the height asserted while in range, a shipped-band control leg; the one-Flak leg (measured 0 % survive) is GD-34's, with `flak_gun.spread_deg` freed from the +-15 % anchor latitude (owner default 1.2 deg)

**Evidence.**

1. **Measured under valid preconditions:** 50 seeds each, Flak alive / finished / `supply01` 1.0 / ammo / power balanced, Bomber loaded, first seen 1,588-1,600 m, in `range_m` with bombs: one Flak **0 / 50 release** (band >= 60 %), two Flak **49 / 50 die before release** (band >= 80 %, met). Floors in the JSON: 0 and 80.
2. **Why 0 %, exactly as A-128 (2a) predicted.** Every turret solves a straight-line intercept with no gravity term (02 s4.4 Aim, R0 B.5) and the pinned Bomber flies level at constant 70 m/s, so the solution is exact; the only miss source is `spread_deg` 0.5, which is 8.7 m at 1,000 m against a 25 m `blast_radius_m` with the 12 m early-burst fuse. Every salvo hits: 60 per 2 s = 30 DPS from one Flak, 300 HP in 10 s, well inside the 12.4 s run. 02 s10.4's row assumes "~50 % hits"; nothing in the content produces a 50 % miss rate.
3. **The lever and its size.** For a per-shell miss distance that is 2-D Gaussian with sigma s, P(within 25 m) = 1 - exp(-25^2 / 2 s^2) = 0.5 gives s = 21.2 m; at the mean engagement range of the run (~1,065 m, from 1,500 m in to the release ~630 m short) that is 1.14 deg, so ~1.2 deg is where 02 s10.4's arithmetic (15 DPS per Flak: 186 damage from one, 372 from two) holds. A-128's +-15 % latitude caps the spread at 0.575 deg, which cannot reach it. 02 s4.1 marks spread *(anchor)*; the 01-fixed Flak values are "30 in a 25 m burst at 300 m/s to 1,500 m" (damage, blast, speed, range), none of which this touches. Range-scaled dispersion or a Bomber evade policy would be new mechanisms (02 / 05 text, a 05 policy); the spread is a value that already exists for this purpose.
4. **Cross-effects to check, not to guess:** `flak_gun` is "0" (even) against the Dart and Lancer and "++" against the Bomber and the loaded Hauler in 02 s10.1; BAL-12 bounds the Flak's Ore share. A wider spread lowers Flak lethality against every air target; GD-34 reads those bands after the change.

**Decision.**

- **C-06 setup (GD-26 round 2; 02 s18.3 row patched here):** real `BomberBrain` ATTACK against a Refinery guarded by one, then two, finished, powered and supplied `flak_battery` driven by the real `TowerBrain`, 50 fixed seeds each, **the brain's `deck_alt_agl_m` / `band_low_agl_m` / `band_high_agl_m` pinned to 600 m by the rig**. Preconditions asserted first and on every seed: the Flak alive, finished, `supply01` 1.0, ammo, defender power balanced; the Bomber loaded; defenders first see the Bomber at >= 1,500 m and <= `sensor_radius` + one tick of travel; the Bomber enters `flak_gun` `range_m` with bombs aboard; **and while inside `range_m` its height over the first Flak stays inside [`h_rel_min`, `h_rel_max`] and near 600 m, with the tolerance derived from the brain's altitude law (`kp_alt` / `vz_max_mps`), not a free number** (the review's root cause). Band met -> green; else the JSON floors (`one_flak_survive_min`, `two_flak_die_min`) at the measured rate minus 10 points, floored at 0, the measured rates in the PR body and the 02 row.
- **Control leg C-06c (shipped bands, >= 10 seeds, one Flak, no Autocannon):** `AttackRun` band DECK at R2, height over the Flak below `h_rel_min` 150 while inside `range_m`, the Flak fires 0 shells, the stick is released, first-seen distance recorded; the numbers pinned under `measured.shipped_bands` in the JSON and the `_doc` states that `bomber_rel_m` overrides the brain's bands (review checklist 2-3). It trips when sim_ai or content moves the altitude.
- **02 s18.3 C-06 row** reads "**measured 0 / 98 %, band open: GD-34**" with the cause in one clause (patched here). 02 s10.4 row 1 gains "(~50 % hits is the design assumption; GD-26 measured every salvo hitting at `spread_deg` 0.5; GD-34 retunes the spread, A-139)".
- **GD-34 (acceptance_append_replace):** make C-06 pass by data with **`flak_gun.spread_deg` as the primary lever, freed from the +-15 % anchor latitude** (OWNER default **1.2 deg**; sweep 0.8-1.6 and pick the value where the one-Flak leg lands in 60-75 % and the two-Flak leg stays >= 80 %), then `flak_shell.fuse_delay_s`, `flak_gun` `salvo` / `reload_s`, Bomber `hp` / `v_cruise` inside +-15 %; `damage` 30, `blast_radius_m` 25, `speed_mps` 300 and `range_m` 1,500 do not move (01-fixed, 02 s4.1); restore the JSON floors to 60 / 80; re-check BAL-12 and the Flak columns of 02 s10.1 at the new spread; record the change in the 02 revision (s4.1 row, s10.4 row). If no spread in the sweep reaches both legs, file the design change (range-scaled dispersion or a 05 Bomber evade policy) as a contract question with the sweep numbers, as A-128 already says. GD-34 does **not** own C-08 (A-140).

**Not accepted:** amending the 02 s18.3 band numbers now (one measurement at one spread; the band is the design intent and the lever exists); widening `blast_radius_m` or the fuse to miss (they are 01-fixed or make the Flak *more* lethal); a `spread_deg` change inside GD-26 (a test task does not retune content; 10 s4.3 table); a C-06 that counts "released" without "survived" or vice versa (unchanged from A-128).

## A-140 (GD-26 C-08, review question 2) C-08's band is a side-symmetry assertion over decided duels and is not GD-34's; the Dart-vs-Dart lethality finding is sim_ai's: new AI-46 (Opus); setup 1,200 m / 60 s / >= 80 decided (owner defaults); GD-26 keeps the 600 m / 15 s tripwire until AI-46

**Evidence.**

1. **What 02 asks.** 02 s18.3 C-08: "Dart vs Dart, both autopilot, 200 seeds | each side wins 45-55 %". It is a *fairness* tripwire (no side, slot-order or spawn-order advantage in a symmetric fight), which is why GD-26 swaps sides and spawn order on odd seeds. 02 names no cap and no separation; 45-55 % only means anything over *decided* duels with a sample large enough that +-5 points is not noise (n = 80 gives a +-11 point 95 % interval, n = 200 +-7; the band is loose by design, the sample must not be).
2. **What GD-26 measured.** 200 seeds, 600 m apart nose to nose at 300 m AGL, real `DartBrain` AUTO on both: all 200 close inside `fire_range_m` 650, 85 have a shot, **487 rounds in total (2.4 per duel), 199 / 200 draw at 15 s** (team 0: 1 win); 45 s: 191 / 200 draw; 60 s: 36 / 40. A Dart has 100 HP against 6-14 per hit, so a kill needs ~10 hits; 02 s11.2 sets the autopilot's gunnery goal at "12-18 % hits vs a manoeuvring Dart" (`aim_error_deg` 1.2, `spread_deg` 0.6 against a 6 m bounding radius), so a decided duel needs ~60-80 rounds *fired at the lead*, i.e. 6-8 s inside the fire cone, 25-30x what the pair spends. The failure is not the cap: it is that two symmetric AUTO Darts hardly fire at each other.
3. **Where that lives.** The fire gate (`angle(nose, L) < atan(fire_cone_k 1.5 x r_target / dist)`, dist < 650), the A-93 standoff (200 m) and break-off (gap < closing speed x 2.0 s: two Darts closing at 220 m/s break off at a 440 m gap, inside fire range), EXTEND (2 s straight, a 90 deg break when the target is ahead) and the 3 Hz think are all 05 s6.2 `DartBrain` rules (AI-13, Opus). A head-on symmetric merge where both ships break off at the same tick and mirror each other is the plausible mechanism; a sim-side hit-registration fault is the other, and the same experiment separates them (rounds fired at the lead vs hits). Neither is a def value GD-34 can turn (there is no BAL band for Dart-vs-Dart lethality and A-128 gave GD-34 only C-06), and neither is GD-26's (a test task). SC-28 (Dart kills a Bomber in <= 60 s, 10 / 10) shows the gun works against a non-manoeuvring target; the gap is the manoeuvring case 02 s11.2 prices.
4. **Runtime.** C-08 costs 35 s hosted at 15 s x 200 draws; at 60 s mostly drawn it would cost ~140 s and break GD-26's "< 2 min total". A decided duel ends at the kill, so once the Darts are lethal the 60 s cap costs little; the setup change therefore lands with the fix, not with GD-26.

**Decision.**

- **C-08's band is a symmetry assertion over decided duels** (02 s18.3 row patched here): "each side wins 45-55 % of the decided duels, >= 80 of 200 decided" (OWNER default 80). Setup (OWNER defaults): the pair **1,200 m apart** nose to nose (inside both `sensor_radius` 1,400 so each sees the other at tick 0, outside `fire_range_m` 650 + the 100 m ENGAGE entry so the brain flies the merge), **300 m AGL**, odd seeds swap sides and spawn order, **cap 60 s** (Dart `fuel_s` 240 bounds any duel; GD-26's 45 / 60 s runs show the cap is not the limiter).
- **GD-26 round 2 keeps the measured 600 m / 15 s / 200-seed tripwire** with the counts pinned, `band_open` reading **"AI-46"** (not GD-34) and the 45 / 60 s runs cited in `_doc` (review nit); the test keeps asserting "band open" while fewer than `min_decided` duels decide and the band once they do.
- **New AI-46** (sim_ai, Opus, 1.0 d, M3, after AI-13 and GD-26): diagnose the symmetric merge on the C-08 rig (rounds fired per duel, rounds fired while the lead was inside the cone, hits, per-round hit rate against the 02 s11.2 12-18 % goal, median time to the first kill, EXTEND / break-off counts per duel), fix it in `DartBrain` within 05 s6.2's vocabulary (any new or changed `autopilots` key is a B.1 append with a 05 s6.2 sentence), move C-08 to the A-140 setup and assert the band; **>= 80 of 200 duels decide within 60 s** and each side wins 45-55 % of them; SC-01, SC-28, SC-29 and the `test_dart_perf.gd` bounds unchanged; C-08 under 60 s hosted. If rounds leave at the lead with the hit rate inside the goal and the duels still draw, the brain is the cause; if rounds leave at the lead and do not register, AI-46 files a sim BUG task with the numbers instead of touching the brain. 02 s11.2's hit-rate goal gains no new number here; AI-46 reports against it.
- **GD-34** loses the C-08 pointer (its acceptance never had it; the JSON's `band_open` was GD-26's guess).

**Not accepted:** reading the 45-55 % band over all 200 duels including draws (a 0.5 % win rate is not a side bias, it is no fight); raising `aim_error_deg` / `fire_cone_k` / `spread_deg` in content to force kills before the diagnosis (02 s11.2's 12-18 % goal is the design and the measured hit rate is unknown); a C-08 that scripts one Dart (02 says both autopilot; the symmetric case is the point); giving C-08 to GD-34 (no data lever, no BAL band).
