## A-109 The RAM exemption for own-team pad structures is the deck half-space, not the approach column

**Evidence.**

1. **The measured geometry.** `WorldDefs.radius_of` (`world_defs.gd:147`, "interim until Build, TA-19, sizes structures"; TA-19 is done and still calls it, `build.gd:125`) gives a structure the half-diagonal of its footprint on the GRID_BUILD grid: one cell is `PLANET_RADIUS * pi / 2 / 1024` = 9.204 m, so the Core (8x8) is a **52.07 m** sphere, the Outpost (6x6) 39.05 m, Pad / Hangar / Relay Station (4x4) 26.03 m, Refinery and Vehicle Bay 39.05 m, Shipyard (8x6) 46.02 m, Reactor (5x5) 32.5 m. The sphere is centred on `pos` (the pad plane, 03 s6.1), so it reaches `radius` metres straight up and sideways. 07 s4.2 (07:301) gives the Core's real body as a 44 x 44 x 3 m slab, a central tower to 30 m and a 24 m ring pad deck around the tower base: the sphere over-reaches the slab by 21 m sideways and the tower by 22 m up.
2. **Every landing crosses the sphere outside the column.** 05 s6.4's L4-L6 (05:400-401) transition from `transition_agl_m` 60 at 250 m to `hover_agl_m` 25 over the pad; at 25 m above the pad plane the Core's sphere reaches sqrt(52.07^2 - 25^2) = **45.7 m** sideways while the A-53 column is 12 m wide. The autopilot and any human pilot who does not arrive vertically over the pad centre at > 45 m altitude spend about 33 m of horizontal travel inside RAM volume. The AI-14 tests prove it: `Ai14Kit.core` (`godot/test/sim/ai/ai14_kit.gd:121`) shrinks the Core's `radius` to its `pad_radius_m` so the landing tests pass, and `sim_ai/brains/MODULE.md:168` records it as BUG-1.
3. **The precedent.** A-87 (B.13) already exempts an own-team ship from the Shipyard's whole sphere, DOCKED or not, above or below the deck, because "a departing ship sits inside its sphere"; A-82 (4) exempts a DOCKED ship against its `link`. A-53 was the smallest rule that let a vertical landing happen through a sphere "of any radius > 0.5 m"; it was written before the 52 m radius was measured. A-109 is the same trade A-87 made, bounded one way tighter (below the deck still rams).
4. **Narrower shapes do not solve it.** A cone or glide corridor (offset <= `pad_radius_m` + k * altitude) needs a k that admits the 05 transition (k >= 1.6 for the Core at hover height) and would still kill a pilot who circles the pad at 20 m before settling; a footprint box with a height (`height_m`, 07 s4.2) keeps the Core's 30 m tower above the 25 m hover, so the horizontal move from the transition point to the column top still rams; a Core-specific shape leaves the Outpost (39 m sphere, 10 m pad: 37.7 m reach at hover height) with the same bug. The exemption is needed for every pad-bearing structure whatever the body shape becomes; the body shape is a separate problem (A-110).
5. **What the rule costs.** An own-team ship can fly through the body of its own Core, Outpost, Pad, Hangar or Relay Station above the pad plane without a `RAM`. 01:125 and 02:234 give ramming no purpose other than destruction; 02:235's "ramming ignores teams" is already carved out by A-82 and A-87; projectile test 8 (03 s6.1) has no occlusion, so a ship hiding inside its own Core gains no cover; towers and brains do not occlude either. The residual is cosmetic (a ship clipping through its own building) and is what the Shipyard already shows under A-87.

**Decision.** **Ratified exactly as PR #114 states it, as a widening of A-53.** In `_ship_structure` (03 s6.1 ship vs structure row), for a ship `s` with collision radius `r` and a structure `o` of the same team whose def has `pad_radius_m` > 0: if `s` is DOCKED, A-82 (4) applies (`link[s] == o` exempts); if `s` is not DOCKED, let `alt = (pos[s] - pos[o]) . up[o]` be its altitude above the pad plane (the plane through `pos[o]` with normal `up[o]`; GD-15 may add a deck offset and the rule follows the plane); **`s` is never RAM-tested against `o` when `alt >= -(r + 0.5)`, at any horizontal offset.** Below that plane the sphere-sphere test applies. Enemy ships are RAM-tested against the whole sphere at every altitude. Structures with `pad_radius_m` 0 (Refinery, Reactor, Vehicle Bay, Solar Array, Ice Extractor, Relay Pylon, Sensor Mast, towers) keep the full test for every team (02:235 stands for them); the Shipyard keeps A-87. The landing volume (03 s6.4; `C_PAD`) is unchanged: horizontal offset <= `pad_radius_m` and |`alt`| <= `r + 0.5`; the nearest-pad choice (`_pad_slot`, `_pad_off`) is unchanged. Contract tests: `test_collision.gd::test_core_sized_sphere_does_not_ram_a_friendly_ship_above_the_deck` (52 m sphere, own ship at offset 20 m / altitude 30 m: 0 RAM; below the deck: 1 RAM; enemy above the deck: 1 RAM), `test_pad_free_structure_still_rams_a_friendly_ship_above_its_deck`, and the moved "beside the column" case of `test_approach_column_exempts_friendly_descent_from_ram_but_not_enemy_or_outsiders` (now beside the column and below the deck: 1 RAM). **Not accepted:** a Core-specific footprint shape (leaves the Outpost broken); a cone or approach corridor (a tuned constant with no measurement behind it, and a pilot orbiting low over the pad still dies); exempting every own-team structure above its deck (02:235 would lose its meaning for the Refinery and Reactor with no landing need to justify it; the Hauler over-flight of its own Refinery at 30 m AGL is the shape bug of A-110, which hits both teams and is fixed once there).

## A-110 The interim footprint sphere is the root cause; its replacement is GD-42, the AI-14 workaround removal is AI-43

**Evidence.** Evidence 1 of A-109: every structure body is a sphere as tall as it is wide, so an enemy Dart 45 m over a Core, a Hauler 30 m over its own Refinery (39 m sphere, AI-15 "descend to the wreck at 30 m AGL", 05 s6.5) or anyone 25 m over a Reactor takes a `RAM` where 07's models have 3 m slabs and 9-30 m halls. A-109 removes the landing case for own ships only; the vertical over-reach is a both-teams bug that BUG-1's acceptance does not name. The fix needs a per-structure height, which no def carries (B.1 `structures.json`; 07 s4.2 recipes have the numbers): a new required field is 10 s4.1 "Change" and a task, not a review finding. BUG-1's acceptance also requires "AI-14's test workaround that shrinks the Core radius is removed", but `ai14_kit.gd` is under `godot/test/sim/ai/` (the sim_ai lane's tests; 10 s4.3) and PR #114 rightly leaves it alone.

**Decision.** (1) **GD-42** (sim, Sonnet, 1.0 d, M4, after BUG-1): `StructureDef.height_m` becomes a **required** `structures.json` / `towers.json` field (the body top above the pad plane, from the 07 s4.2 recipe tables; schema `{number, minimum 1, maximum 100}` with a `$comment` citing A-110; canonical fixture updated) and the structure body for the ship-vs-structure test becomes a **footprint puck**: horizontal offset <= `radius` (the half-diagonal stays; TA-19 footprints are squares and rectangles, the puck errs on the safe side at the corners) and -(`r` + 0.5) <= `alt` <= `height_m` + `r`; projectile test 8 keeps the sphere until measured otherwise (a puck test per projectile per entity is the hot path of 03 s4.2 step 8). A-109 stays in force after GD-42 (evidence 4 of A-109). **OWNER:** M4 (default) vs M3. (2) **AI-43** (sim_ai, Haiku, 0.1 d, M3, after BUG-1): delete the `radius` override in `Ai14Kit.core`, drop the BUG-1 sentence from `sim_ai/brains/MODULE.md:168`, and run SC-21 and the AI landing tests with the real 52.07 m Core radius; BUG-1's acceptance is rewritten so the removal lives here. (3) **02 s4.3 rule 8** gains the clause "except a ship against its own team's `launch_pad` or pad structures (R0 A-82, A-87, A-109)"; 01:125 is unchanged (it names no teams).
