## A-81 (review question 2) `SET_RALLY` and `TOGGLE_AUTO_REPLENISH` are team-shared; owner-only is `CANCEL_*` only

**Evidence.** 01 s4.4 rule 5 (01:237): "Every teammate can order any team unit and build with the team stockpile. Production queues are team-shared." 02 s6.4 (02:395) names exactly one owner-only kind: "**`CANCEL_PRODUCE` is owner-only**"; its rally bullet (02:400) and auto-replenish bullet (02:398) carry no owner clause. R0 C3-08 (R0:123) narrows `CANCEL_PRODUCE` and `CANCEL_BUILD` and nothing else. 04 s11.3 V-OWN (04:1164) is the only place the four kinds appear together, and it is explicit: the row-wide check is "target alive and `team(target) == link.team` (team-shared command, 01 s4.4 rule 5)"; the owner-only sentence inside the row begins "**Owner-only cancel (R0 C3-08):** `CANCEL_BUILD` and `CANCEL_PRODUCE`". 04 s2.3 rows 214 and 220 cite V-OWN for `SET_RALLY` and `TOGGLE_AUTO_REPLENISH` without the "(owner-only cancel)" qualifier that rows 211 and 213 carry. 05 s7.1 principle 1 (05:668) describes the Commander bot's rights the same way: `SET_RALLY, TOGGLE_AUTO_REPLENISH` unqualified, "`CANCEL_BUILD` and `CANCEL_PRODUCE` on its own items only".

**Decision.** The reviewer is right. `SET_RALLY` and `TOGGLE_AUTO_REPLENISH` are **team-shared**: valid when the target factory is alive, finished (not `F_BUILDING`), of a producing class, and `team(target) == team(issuer)`; a teammate is `ACCEPTED`; a foreign-team or dead target is `REJECTED_NOT_OWNER` with the strike rule of 04:1164 (strike only for a foreign-team target). Owner-only (queuer / placer, or any teammate when that player is disconnected, eliminated or a bot) applies to `CANCEL_BUILD` and `CANCEL_PRODUCE` and to no other `Command.Kind`. The TA-18 interim `_check_own_structure` was a placeholder until GD-17; it does not set precedent.

**Clarification that falls out of 02:398.** Auto-replenish targets are **per player**. A `TOGGLE_AUTO_REPLENISH` names a factory only to pass V-OWN and to resolve the team; its roster replaces the **issuer's** own targets, never the factory owner's or a teammate's. The scan then appends into the least-loaded eligible queue of the team (02:398), so the factory in the command is not binding for placement either. `sim/production/MODULE.md` P9 says this in one sentence.

**Alternatives.** Owner-only for all four kinds (the PR as submitted): contradicts 01:237 and 04:1164 and would need a C3-08 widening, which the griefing analysis of 04 s11.5 (04:1205) already rejected: "possession and orders stay team-shared by design (01 s4.4)". A rally point is an order.

## A-82 (review root cause, instances 1-3) The launch is the B.5 `LAUNCHED` rule from the socket; no parked timer; the docked exemption keys on `link`

**Evidence.** 02 s2.3 (02:154): a produced ship is "parked tail-down on its Shipyard's launch pad, and is airborne (> 20 m AGL) about 3 s after completion ... the ship spawns DOCKED on the socket and leaves through the normal `LAUNCHED` rule". R0 B.9 A-19 (R0:443): "produced ships spawn DOCKED on the launch socket and leave through the B.5 `LAUNCHED` rule ... The socket position is 07's (`presentation`), not a sim field." R0 B.5 (R0:324): "`LAUNCHED` sets `vel = launch_speed_mps * up_pad` (tail-down launch along the pad normal) and the autopilot or pilot pitches over above 20 m AGL"; the landing volume is "altitude above the pad surface <= `bounding_radius + 0.5`". 03 s6.1 (03:770): "docked ships are exempt against their own pad".

**Decision.**

1. **Where the ship is parked (sim).** Because the socket is not a sim field (A-19), the sim's socket is the structure origin on the structure's local up: `pos = pos[factory] + up[factory] * radius_of(ship)`. That is the same height band B.5 uses for a landed ship (within `bounding_radius + 0.5` of the pad surface), and it is < 20 m AGL for every ship class, so a parked ship is **not** airborne by 02's own definition. `vel = 0`, `link = factory`, `DOCKED`, `fwd` along the pad normal (tail-down), like a landing. `presentation` places the mesh on the 07 socket; the sim does not care.
2. **How it leaves.** On the completion tick the ship spawns DOCKED at the socket; on the **next tick** the normal B.5 `LAUNCHED` rule fires (`vel = launch_speed_mps * up_pad`, `LAUNCHED(a ship, b factory)`, DOCKED cleared), and the autopilot climbs and pitches over above 20 m AGL. "About 3 s" is the climb under `launch_speed_mps` 5, `a_thrust`, gravity and the autopilot's launch law, not a hold. **No `launch_delay_s` and no hold timer.** A ship that dies while DOCKED never launches; a ship possessed while DOCKED (05 SC-17, 05:1004) launches when the pilot thrusts, not by the forced rule: `LaunchBay` skips a `POSSESSED` ship and lets 05's launch routine own it (the GD-15 pad launch is the same rule; whichever merges second reuses the first).
3. **Acceptance reading of "Dart airborne 23 s after its start at full power"** (09 GD-17): `test_launch_bay.gd` asserts (a) the first tick at which `(|pos| - PLANET_RADIUS - terrain_height) > 20` lies in `[completion + 1 tick, completion + 4 s]`, i.e. 20 s build + "about 3 s" with 1 s slack, because 02:154 says "about" and no doc fixes the climb to the tick; (b) while DOCKED the ship's altitude above the pad surface is <= `bounding_radius + 0.5` (B.5 band); (c) exactly one `LAUNCHED` event. The PR's perf note records the **measured** climb time to 20 m AGL. If it lands outside [2 s, 4 s] the author reports it and the acceptance string is re-baselined at PM-01 against the measurement (09 s6), not by widening the test.
4. **Collision exemption (03 s6.1 erratum).** "own pad" in 03:770 means the ship's `link` structure. A DOCKED ship is never RAM-tested against `link`, whether `link` has `pad_radius_m` > 0 (service pad) or is a `launch_pad: true` structure with `pads` 0 (Shipyard, A-19). `collision.gd` `_ship_structure` therefore tests `docked and link[s] == id_of(o)` **before** the `pad_r > 0.0` gate; the A-53 approach-column exemption (R0:491) stays gated on `pad_r > 0` because a launch pad never accepts a landing. Consequence: `PARK_CLEARANCE_M` is deleted (no sim constant, no def field); `LocalSimView.pad_occupant(shipyard)` may return the parked ship while it is DOCKED for that one tick (it is `link`); `nearest_friendly_pad` still never returns a Shipyard (A-19). 03:770 is patched in this PR.

**Alternatives.** Park outside the bounding sphere with a 3 s timer (the PR): the ship is airborne by 02's definition while DOCKED, needs a sim constant, and makes "3 s" a parked hold that the climb then adds to. Hold the ship DOCKED for 3 s, then launch, with the socket on the ground: readable, but it is not what 02:154 / A-19 say, and 05's autopilot gets no say in the climb. Both rejected.

## A-83 (review question 1) `launch_delay_s` rejected; `auto_replenish_period_s` ratified as required, 1 s

**Decision.**

| Field | Ruling | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `economy.json` `launch_delay_s` | **Rejected.** Remove from `economy.json`, `economy.schema.json`, `EconomyDef`, the canonical fixture, the `_doc` string and `sim/production/MODULE.md`. | - | A-82: there is no hold; the number 3 is a climb time 02:154 describes, not a rule constant. A def field for a rule that does not exist is not ADR-09. |
| `economy.json` `auto_replenish_period_s` | **Ratified, required**, bounds (0, 10], `$comment` "02 s6.4 auto-replenish scan period; 1 Hz anchor (A-83), GD-34 may retune". `EconomyDef.auto_replenish_period_s`, `AutoReplenish._period = max(1, ceil(period * tick_hz))` ticks. | **1** | 02 s6.4 (02:398) states the rule ("when alive + queued < target, the item is appended") but gives no cadence; the scan must have one, and it is sim-affecting (changes the tick an item is queued on), so under ADR-09 it is a def, not a const. Required rather than optional: every other `economy.json` rule constant is required (A-66, A-79, A-80 precedents); an optional field with a code default re-creates the literal the rule forbids. Placement in `economy.json`: it is a production rule, and `rules.json` holds nothing that belongs to a unit, weapon, structure or mode (A-51 / A-65 reading of B.1). 1 s is an anchor: once per second a player's deficit is closed by one item, which keeps the 2 Hz `TEAM_DETAIL` replication (04 s2.3) able to show every append, costs ~0 per tick, and is faster than the Commander / Adjutant think cadence that sets the targets (05 s7, s9). |

**OWNER:** the one required field and its value; default applied. The content hash changes (every def-field addition does).

**Consequences for PR #67.** `economy.json` carries `auto_replenish_period_s` only; the `_doc` sentence becomes "02 s6.4 (auto_replenish_period_s: seconds between auto-replenish scans, A-83)". R0 B.1 `economy.json` line gains `auto_replenish_period_s` 1 (patched in this PR). No backlog change; no other lane consumes either field.
