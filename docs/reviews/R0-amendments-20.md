## A-136 (PM-05) A replicated projectile's death reaches the client as its `ROSTER_REMOVE` (<= 3 ticks + latency); until then it is dead-reckoned for at most 3 ticks and then held `stale`; the KEYFRAME encoder returns empty above `RECORDS_MAX`

**Evidence.** (1) 04 s3.6: a SNAPSHOT lists only entities whose record changed or whose tier is due this tick; an entity absent from a snapshot is "unchanged or lower-tier", so absence carries no death information. (2) 04 s5.6 / `net/client/MODULE.md`: `NetPose` dead-reckons a roster entry for at most 3 ticks past its last record and then freezes it with `stale`; `NetSimView.alive(id)` reads the roster, which the client edits only on `ENTITY_META` and `ROSTER_REMOVE` (channel 3). (3) A-132 delays the `ROSTER_REMOVE` by 3 ticks after the sim death, so the earliest tick on which a client can know is death + 3 ticks + one-way latency; the sentence "alive is false from the first tick without a record" in amendments 19 described the server's roster bookkeeping, not the client's view, and the NET-58 review caught it. (4) `KeyframeCodec.encode` (`keyframe_codec.gd:95-96`) returns `PackedByteArray()` when more than `RECORDS_MAX` records qualify and leaves the mirror untouched; `net/keyframe/MODULE.md` says the same; nothing asserts. `RECORDS_MAX` is computed as `(65,535 - 10) / 23` (the 22 B worst-case record plus one spare byte) = 2,848, the same number 04 row 0x86 states with a different derivation.

**Decision.** Text only, no code change (the as-built PR #133 behaviour is the contract):

- 04 s4.1 projectile paragraph, the "roster bookkeeping only" sentence reads: "The hold is roster bookkeeping only: the client learns of the death from the `ROSTER_REMOVE` (<= 3 ticks + one-way latency after the sim death); until it arrives the projectile is dead-reckoned for at most 3 ticks past its last record and then held `stale` (`NetPose`), because a missing record also means unchanged or lower-tier and cannot be read as a death; `NetSimView.alive(id)` is false from the `ROSTER_REMOVE`, nothing renders it after that, and the hold stays below the 1 s held-record timeout."
- R0 B.25 A-132 row: the parenthesis "(`NetSimView.alive` is false for the dead projectile from the first tick without a record; nothing renders it)" is corrected by a trailing pointer to this row (rows are append-only; the pointer is the canonical reading). `R0-amendments-19.md` A-132 gains an erratum line.
- 04 s2.3 row 0x86 and R0 B.25 A-134: "the encoder asserts the bound" reads "the encoder returns an empty `PackedByteArray` (no message, the mirror untouched) above it"; the bound's derivation reads "2,848 = (65,535 - 10) / 23: the 22 B worst-case record plus one spare byte per record". `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 1, `test_protocol.gd`'s at-most row and the `test_keyframe_codec` empty-encode row are the contract tests (NET-58).
- `net/interest/MODULE.md` and `net/client/MODULE.md` (NET-58 round 2 / 3) say the same where they describe the hold; no change if they already do.

**Not accepted:** a client-side death inference from record absence (contradicts 04 s3.6 and would kill tier-C entities); a `PROJECTILE_DEATH` event or earlier REMOVE (A-132's hold exists to beat the cross-channel race; shortening it reopens the 1-3 malformed snapshots the PR measured).

## A-137 (GD-09 review round 2) CB13a retry cadence: a tower holding the trigger with nothing engageable retries the pick once per tick on which the weapon is due, spending nothing; at most one envelope scan per tower per tick; scan-count test and perf line named

**Evidence.** (1) The possessed-tower case (02 s11.2 Towers row: "a possessed gunner leads, baits or misses by hand") needs the first round to leave on the tick the hostile enters the envelope; a cadence of one scan per fire window would cost a possessed Autocannon up to 1.2 s, a Flak 2.0 s and a Rail Lance 8.0 s of latency after a failed pick (`weapons.json` `burst_interval_s` / `reload_s`), which the review names a design trade-off and which no 02 text accepts. (2) The idle-held scan is not a per-shot scan: no round leaves, so A-129's cost objection (an O(entities) scan per round of a 5-round burst, 12 rps) does not apply; the scan runs at most once per tower per tick, the same bound a held lock weapon already pays under CB13 (`_seek` calls `_pick` every held tick), so an idle-held gun / flak / rail tower costs exactly what an idle-held `rocket_tower` costs today. (3) AI towers rarely idle-hold: `TowerBrain` holds FIRE1 only while its own `eligible` check (team-visible, in envelope, 05 s6.8) passes, which is stricter than the sim's gate (no visibility term, A-129), so an AI tower's idle-held run is bounded by one think period after its target dies or leaves. The sustained idle-held population is therefore the possessed towers, at most one per player (2-8 players, 02 s1). (4) `SeekerScan.pick` is O(live entities) with three early-outs before the envelope test; on the canonical scene (~1,120 entities, 03 s4.2) one scan is of the order of 0.1-0.5 ms in GDScript, which is why the bound and the measurement below are part of the ruling and why the window-open scan stays once per window. (5) A failed pick spends nothing today (`_burst`: `fired` false leaves `cd_secondary` unchanged; `_weapon`: `c = maxf(c, 0.0)`), so the retry needs no new state and S4 holds; the "re-picks once" acceptance clause describes the failed O(1) re-check inside a window, not the idle case.

**Decision (binding; GD-09 round 3).**

- **Cadence (CB13a restated).** For a gun / flak / rail tower the envelope scan (`_pick`) runs (i) when the fire window opens with the trigger held (burst window reopens, salvo / reload due, first round after a hold), (ii) once, in the same tick, when the held target fails its O(1) re-check at a shot, and (iii) while the trigger is held with `lock_target[s] == 0`, on every tick on which the weapon is due (cooldown within half a tick of 0). **Bound: at most one `_pick` per tower per tick, and none on a tick whose shot re-check passes.** A failed pick spends nothing: no round, no ammo, no supply draw, no cooldown, no window (the next due tick retries), so a hostile entering the envelope is fired on at the first due tick after it does (latency <= 1 tick). The scan is the last check: a tower with too few rounds, a full pool (CB11) or power 0 (CB12a) does not scan.
- **Perf line (A-129's row, made concrete).** The PR body cites, on the pinned runner or the dev laptop named in 03 s4.2 row 9, `fire.step` p95 for three cases on `perf_tick.gd`'s canonical entity count: (a) 32 firing towers with a hostile in the envelope, (b) 32 idle-held towers (FIRE1 held, 20 hostiles in range but outside the envelope, the contract worst case), (c) 8 idle-held towers (the player cap; the realistic sustained worst case per evidence 3). **Gate:** case (c) stays under the row-9 fire share, 0.6 ms (150 firings x 4 us, 03 s4.2 row 9); cases (a) and (b) are reported. Measured, not estimated; the 14-tower 27 us figure does not satisfy the row.
- **Fallback branch (09 s6 style; only if gate (c) fails):** a failed pick sets the weapon's shot cooldown (`cd_primary`) to an optional `weapons.json` `fire.retry_s` (absent or 0 = retry next tick; A-66 pattern), so the retry rides the existing cooldown with no new state and S4 holds; the key is a B.1 append ratified by a one-row amendment with the measured number, canonical value the `TowerBrain` think period (1 / 3 Hz, 05 s10) unless the measurement says otherwise. Not applied unless the gate fails. OWNER (default: per-tick retry, no key).
- **Tests (GD-09 round 3; `test_tower_gates.gd`):** `WeaponFire` exposes a diagnostics counter `scans: int` (incremented in `_pick`, never serialised, not in the S4 bytes, reset by the test; documented in `sim/combat/MODULE.md` as diagnostics, not state). Named rows: (T1) a `gun_turret` with FIRE1 held on an envelope hostile for 72 ticks (two windows, 10 rounds) has `scans == 2` (a per-shot-scan mutant, `_gate(s, k, true)` in `_burst`, gives 10); (T2) the same tower with the hostile outside the envelope for 30 ticks spends 0 rounds, `cd_secondary` stays 0, `scans == 30`, and when the hostile enters the envelope on tick 31 the first round spawns on that tick (a once-per-window mutant gives `scans == 1` and a late first round); (T3) FIRE1 released for 30 ticks: `scans == 0`; (T4) the existing mid-burst leave test asserts `scans == 2` for the window (the pick and the one re-pick); (T5) an idle-held tower with 0 rounds: `scans == 0`. The S4 mid-window byte-exact test is unchanged (`scans` is not state).
- **MODULE.md:** `sim/combat/MODULE.md` CB13a's "rescans every tick (the human worst case, as for a seeker)" sentence reads: "a tower holding the trigger with nothing engageable retries the pick once per due tick, spending nothing (at most one scan per tower per tick, as a held seeker; R0 B.26 A-137)"; the perf sentence cites the three cases above. 02 s4.4 Aim bullet gains "retried once per due tick while the trigger is held and nothing is engageable" (patched here).

**Not accepted:** a once-per-window retry (1.2 / 2 / 8 s of latency for a possessed gunner; contradicts 02 s11.2's hand-fired tower); an every-N-ticks `const` in `WeaponFire` (a sim-affecting number outside defs, ADR-09; the fallback above puts it in `weapons.json` if it is ever needed); a per-shot scan (A-129); counting scans by instrumenting `SeekerScan` with a static (static state in a sim utility breaks S4 reasoning and the determinism review).
