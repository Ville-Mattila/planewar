### B.5 Motion, landing, tick order, signatures

**Projectile rules.** `slug_light`, `slug_heavy`, `flak_shell`, `rail_slug`: straight lines in the world frame, `p += v * dt`, no parallel transport, no gravity, no damping; sent on the wire as `PROJECTILE_BATCH` spawns, never as entities. `bomb`, `siege_shell`: CF-transported, `v += -g * n * dt`, `p += v * dt`, `v` transported; replicated as entities with gravity-aware dead reckoning. `rocket`: constant speed, guided toward its lock target within `turn_rate_dps` and `lock_cone_deg`, transported; entity. `supply_canister` (SL-5): rendezvous behaviour, entity with hp. Lead solvers everywhere: straight-line intercept with the shooter's velocity carried, no gravity term.

**Flight law** (01 s3.1.1 as amended): thrust along body forward `a_thrust * thrust * eff_t`, `eff_t = thrust_efficiency(v_fwd, cruise_now, eff_fade_start) * ceiling_efficiency(alt)`; brake `brake_factor * a_thrust * clamp(v_fwd / 10, 0, 1)` against forward; gravity `-g n`; damping `-right * v_side * k_side - up * v_up * k_up_eff`, `k_up_eff = k_up + (k_up_flight - k_up) * clamp(v_fwd / v_cruise, 0, 1)^2` under `lift_model = speed_scaled` (`damping_only` uses `k_up`); semi-implicit Euler; dive clamp at `v_dive_max` with `overspeed_dps` flat hull damage; then parallel transport of `v`, `fwd`, `up`; then terrain probe. Free-fall and terminal-sink numbers of 01 are unchanged (k_up at zero forward speed).

**Landing rule** (O-2). A ship lands on friendly pad P when, in one tick: `|p_ship - p_pad| <= pad_radius_m` horizontally in the pad frame; altitude above the pad surface <= `bounding_radius + 0.5`; `-v.dot(up_pad) <= landing_safe_v_vert` (8); tangential speed <= `landing_safe_v_hor` (15); **`fwd.dot(up_pad) >= cos(landing_safe_pitch_deg)` (thrust axis within 25 degrees of the pad normal, nose up)**; pad is friendly and not servicing another ship. Between safe and damage limits (15 / 25 m/s, or attitude 25-45 degrees) hull damage `hp_max * max(ratio_vert, ratio_hor, ratio_tilt)` where each ratio = excess / (damage - safe); beyond, destroyed and the pad takes 100 damage. On landing: `DOCKED`, `vel = 0`, `pos` snapped to the pad socket, `link = pad`, `LANDING` event with the grade (B.3), then `DOCKED` event; service per 02 s2.3; `LAUNCHED` sets `vel = launch_speed_mps * up_pad` (tail-down launch along the pad normal) and the autopilot or pilot pitches over above 20 m AGL. Auto-dock: `Assist.auto_dock()` runs 05's L4-L6 (TRANSITION, HOVER-DESCENT, FLARE) while DOCK is held within `auto_dock_radius_m` 150 of a free friendly pad, on server and in prediction; a landing counts as manual if no assist tick occurred in the final 150 m. Ships have tail landing struts (`HP_Gear_0..2`); the Hangar roof and pad socket geometry assume a nose-up parked pose.

**Tick order** (`SimWorld.step()`, normative): 1 apply queued `Command`s (incl. POSSESS/RELEASE handoffs by `ControlMachine`); 2 store this tick's human `PilotInput`s into input slots; 3 **AI hook** `SimAiHook.on_tick(tick, view, sink)`: think slice (1/10 autopilots, Commander slices) + act for every AI-driven entity, writing input slots through `CommandSink.drive` (refused for POSSESSED); 4 **assist substitution**: `Assist.auto_dock()` / `Assist.link_bridge()` overwrite input slots where active; 5 kinematics: `step_ship` for ships, `GroundMotion.step` for ground, turret slew for towers; 6 projectiles (straight / ballistic / guided / rendezvous); 7 spatial rebuild (counting sort); 8 collision: projectile vs entity sweeps, 8b projectile vs terrain (relief prefilter), ship vs terrain classification (destroy / damage / land / scrape), ship vs ship, ship vs structure; 9 combat: weapon fire, damage rolls, flak bursts, shields, deaths (deferred free), wrecks; 10 economy / production / build / docking service / salvage / Core shield & Exposure timers / caps / scoring (LandingGrade); 11 vision (10 Hz staggered, 5 Hz under cut rung 2); 12 events drained by the caller (`ServerSession` builds snapshots from `SnapshotCodec.prepare_tick`). `ServerSession.tick()` = poll transports -> queue commands/inputs -> `world.step()` -> route events -> snapshots -> housekeeping; it never calls AI directly.
