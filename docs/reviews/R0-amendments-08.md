## A-88 (BLOCKED clause) the steep-dive acceptance counts recoverable starts; the 2 km leg is the harness definition

**Evidence.** 09 / `backlog.json` AI-38 acceptance: "damping_only steep low dives terrain contacts <= 1 % of 200 (was ~5.5 %)". The "~5.5 %" came from the AI-06 harness (PR #64 note), whose waypoint line was 5 % of the way to a random far point, 63-600 m; `TerrainFlightRig` stops at its last waypoint (150 m radius), so about 40 of the 200 dives ended at tick 0 and more before the dive was decided (`sweep_scenarios.gd` `steep_dive` doc, README "AI-38"). The number the acceptance was written against was therefore measured on runs that mostly did not reach the ground or a recovery. On the 2 km line (12 s of flight, the full rig run), the **same 200 start states** give 20 contacts on the pre-AI-38 laws and 17 after AI-38; on the old line 11 -> 10 (README table; `reports/ai38/{before,after}_{legacy,new}_sweep.csv`, confirm rows: `dive_hits` 17, `dive_unrecoverable` 17, `dive_hits_recoverable` 0 for the recommended set under `damping_only`; 0 / 0 / 0 under `speed_scaled`).

The bound: `SweepScenarios.dive_recoverable` flies each start open-loop from tick 0 at a fixed nose elevation 0, 5, ..., 90 degrees in the velocity's vertical plane (wings level, held through `track_thrust_vector` at the class's own rates, thrust 1 with the nose above the horizon and 0 below it, 6 s or until climbing nose-up) and once more with `Assist.pull_up`; a start none of the 20 runs saves is **unrecoverable**: no wings-level autopilot that starts pulling at tick 0 can do better than the best of those, and the PR also tried a knife-edge pull on the side damping, which saves none. Under `damping_only` 17 of 200 starts are unrecoverable (10-45 degree dives from 100-300 m at 0.7-1.1 v_cruise: the steep, low, slow corner, where the Dart's thrust-limited lift under `damping_only` cannot produce the radial acceleration in the height left); under `speed_scaled` none. After AI-38 every remaining contact is one of the 17; **0 of the 183 recoverable starts** meet the ground. The clause as written is unmeetable by any controller under `damping_only`, so it measures the start distribution, not the controller.

**Decision.**

1. **Acceptance re-baselined (not widened).** The AI-38 clause reads: *"gain_sweep `--given=recommended_gains.json --stages=confirm`, 200 seeds, seed 606, 2 km dive leg: `damping_only` steep-low-dive terrain contacts among recoverable starts (`dive_hits_recoverable`) <= 1 % of the recoverable starts (<= 1 of 183), with `dive_unrecoverable` <= 17 of 200 reported beside it; `speed_scaled` `dive_hits` 0 of 200."* A start is **unrecoverable iff `SweepScenarios.dive_recoverable` is false** (the 19 fixed elevations plus `Assist.pull_up`, from tick 0, as above). The classification is ship- and lift-model-specific: it is set by the `ShipDef` rates and thrust, `rules.json` gravity and the lift law, and touches the candidate's `FlightGains` only through the attitude loop that holds a fixed open-loop command (`kp_att` / `kp_roll`), so a candidate cannot improve its score by steering starts into the unrecoverable bucket; `dive_unrecoverable` is a reported column, the confirm stage prints it per candidate, and a run where it differs from the baseline's (17 for the Dart under `damping_only`; a `ShipDef` / `rules.json` / lift-law change) is a re-baseline at PM-01, not a pass or a fail.
2. **The 2 km leg is the harness definition going forward** (`SweepScenarios.DIVE_LEG_M` = 2,000 m; `--dive-leg-m` on the scene). Every future dive number quoted against A-68 / AI-38 / GD-34 is on this line. `--dive-leg-m=0` stays as the comparison replay of the AI-06 line and is never the basis of an acceptance. AI-06's own acceptance ("200 seeds in < 2 min headless") still holds: the confirm rows report `wall_s` 46.8-107.5 s per candidate on the author's laptop.
3. **What the 17 mean for the game.** They are not a flight-model defect to be fixed in `sim/steer`: a Dart under `damping_only` that is 45 degrees nose-down at 100 m and 0.7 v_cruise is dead the moment it gets there, and the law that must keep it from getting there is the brain's pull-up budget (`pull_up_loss`, A-89 below), not PULL_UP itself. The AI-13 acceptance append in the backlog patch makes that the brain's test: a possessed or AUTO Dart under the deck-hold brain never reaches a state `dive_recoverable` classifies as unrecoverable on `cradle` in SC-09 / SC-10 / SC-11.
4. **Not accepted:** changing the start distribution so every start is recoverable (loses the before / after comparability of the 200 states and hides exactly the corner the brain has to avoid), or adding per-model gains (A-68: one law, one set).

**OWNER (default applied):** the <= 1 % of recoverable starts wording and the frozen 17 / 200 report ceiling.

## A-89 (question 2) 05 s5.3 PULL_UP becomes the best-climb law of the lift law; the pull-up budget `pull_up_loss` is defined and ORed with the 1.0 s re-sample

**Evidence.** 05 s5.3 (05:474): "the emergency **PULL_UP** mode starts that tick (nose to local up at max rate, thrust 1, brake off) until TTI >= 4 s"; the `run_safety` sketch (05:213) repeats it. `sim/steer/MODULE.md` before AI-38 (AI-08 finding, now deleted by the PR): "05 s5.3's PULL_UP (nose to local up) kills the lift (`k_up_effective` falls with forward speed), so dives steeper than 30 degrees below ~250 m AGL can still meet the ground; a nose lead of 40-50 degrees off the velocity removed those contacts in a trial (not adopted: 05 law kept)". The reason is in the lift law of 03 s5.2 / R0 B.5: under `speed_scaled` the wing's radial acceleration is proportional to forward speed, so pointing the nose at local up both removes the wing's lift and, at a nose angle past the best climb, spends thrust on killing forward speed; under `damping_only` thrust is the lift and the same geometry applies through `turn_accel`. Measured in PR #75 with the recommended set: `speed_scaled` dive contacts 2 -> 0 (old line) and 8 -> 0 (2 km line); `damping_only` recoverable contacts 3 -> 0 (2 km line); the best-climb elevation converges on a brute-force search within 3 degrees (`test_flight_controller_lift.gd`); seed 104 under `speed_scaled` survives with the best-climb law and hits with nose-to-up (same test file, `test_sweep_scenarios.gd`). No landing or step metric moved the wrong way (200 / 200 docked both models; A-68 limits met with margin: `damping_only` worst 2.30 s / 11.8 %).

**Decision.** 05 s5.3 is amended (patched in this PR, 05:474 and the 05:213 comment) to:

- **PULL_UP attitude law.** The nose goes, at the class's own rates (`FlightController.pull_up`, `shape_rate`), to the elevation in the vertical plane of the velocity that **maximises the radial acceleration available now** (`climb_accel`: the lift law's speed-dependent lift plus thrust along the nose, minus gravity; found by one Newton step per tick, `pull_up_elevation`); the wings roll level (`pull_up_plane`); **thrust 1 while the nose is above the horizon, 0 below it** (below the horizon thrust only steepens the dive); brake off; buttons 0 (`Assist.pull_up`). Exit rule unchanged: TTI >= 4 s (`safety_clear_tti_s`). "Nose to local up" is the limiting case the law chooses only when the speed-dependent lift is zero.
- **Pull-up budget.** `FlightController.pull_up_loss(b, v, up, g)` = the height (m) lost before PULL_UP arrests the sink from the current state: the nose turns from its elevation to `pull_up_elevation` at the pitch-rate cap plus one rate ramp while gravity alone acts, then `climb_accel` at the best climb stops the sink; 0 while climbing; INF when no climb acceleration exists. **PULL_UP trigger (amended):** at act, every 3rd tick, PULL_UP starts when the probe's 1.0 s re-sample is below 0 **or** when the clearance the caller can see (its AGL, or the probe's worst drop-free clearance) is **below `pull_up_loss`**. The AI-06 rig (`TerrainFlightRig.pull_up_budget`) already does both; `Overlays.run_safety` (sim_ai, AI-08) and the brains adopt the OR in **AI-13** (acceptance append below). `Assist.link_bridge` keeps its 1.0 s probe only: AS3 (100 random starts, 0-30 degree dives, no contact) holds and the bridge lasts about one RTT (05 s2.4).
- **No new def key** (A-68: one law; the lift model is read from `Kinematics`, the numbers are the ship's and `rules.json`'s). The `safety_pull_up_tti_s` 2.0 s trigger of 05 s2.4 (05:384; 01 s4.4) is untouched: the budget is a second, earlier trigger, never a later one.
- **05 s10 act row.** `pull_up` 5.2-7.6 us and `pull_up_loss` 5.0-7.5 us run only during a pull-up or its act-time check (every 3rd tick, 05 s5.3 cost line); `track_velocity` with the floor and lead cap 5.4-6.9 us (previously 5.7-7.8). The 6 us act row does not move (A-62: dev-box numbers advisory; TA-22 / AI-30 measure on the pinned runner).

**Alternatives.** Keep "nose to local up" and accept the contacts: rejected; 05 s5.3 is the emergency law, and the measured law dominates it on every scenario under both lift models with no cost elsewhere. A fixed 40-50 degree lead (the AI-08 trial): rejected; it is one point of the best-climb curve and wrong at other speeds (the `best_lead` table shows ~40 degrees for a cruising Dart under `speed_scaled` and > 60 under `damping_only`).

## A-90 (question 3) `flight_common` on `FlightGains.from_dict`, `BridgeParams.from_autopilot`, `OverlayParams.for_ship`; `v` on `Assist.pull_up`; follow-ups

**Evidence.** The best-lift and best-lead tables need the lift law's inputs (`ShipDef`, `RulesDef`, `FlightCommonDef`) at build time, once per class, so that `track_velocity` and `pull_up` allocate nothing per tick (03 s12.2 rules 1-2; 05 s10 act row). `FlightGains.from_dict(flight, def, tick_hz)` had no `RulesDef` or `FlightCommonDef`; `BridgeParams.from_autopilot` and `OverlayParams.for_ship` build a `FlightGains` and had no `FlightCommonDef`. `Assist.pull_up` had no velocity and the best-climb law is a function of it. Callers outside the PR: none (`BridgeParams.table`, `OverlayParams`, `Overlays`, the AI-05 / AI-08 / AI-09 kits and the tuning harness are all in the PR; `net` reaches step 4 through `SimWorld` and never calls these; AI-13 / AI-14 / AI-19 are `todo` and read the MODULE.md as it will be).

**Decision. Ratified** as a 10 s4.1 "Change" with no migration outside the PR:

| Symbol | Before | After (binding) |
|---|---|---|
| `FlightGains.from_dict` | `(flight: Dictionary, def: ShipDef, tick_hz: int) -> FlightGains` | `(flight: Dictionary, def: ShipDef, tick_hz: int, rules_def: RulesDef, flight_common: FlightCommonDef) -> FlightGains` (null: key / def missing) |
| `BridgeParams.from_autopilot` | `(ap, def, rules_def, tick_hz) -> BridgeParams` | `(ap: Dictionary, def: ShipDef, rules_def: RulesDef, tick_hz: int, flight_common: FlightCommonDef) -> BridgeParams` |
| `OverlayParams.for_ship` | `(ap, def, rules, tick_hz) -> OverlayParams` | `(ap: Dictionary, def: ShipDef, rules: RulesDef, tick_hz: int, flight_common: FlightCommonDef) -> OverlayParams` |
| `Assist.pull_up` | `(b: Basis, up_local: Vector3, g: FlightGains, out: PilotInput) -> void` | `(b: Basis, v: Vector3, up_local: Vector3, g: FlightGains, out: PilotInput) -> void` (buttons 0 + `FlightController.pull_up`) |

Parameters are appended, except `v` on `Assist.pull_up`, which sits before `up_local` so the four flight helpers share the `(b, v, up_local, g, out)` order of `FlightController.pull_up`, `pull_up_loss`, `hold_velocity` and `track_velocity`; accepted because every caller is in the PR and `gen_module_api --check` guards it. The new `FlightController` statics (`weight_support_throttle`, `lead_cap`, `best_lead`, `lift_best`, `turn_accel`, `pull_up`, `pull_up_loss`, `pull_up_plane`, `pull_up_elevation`, `climb_accel`), the new `FlightGains` members and the `LEAD_*` constants are appends (10 s4.1 row 1) and need no ruling; FC5 and the AS2 rewording stand as written. **Migration note per consuming lane:** `sim_ai` (AI-13, AI-14, AI-17): build `OverlayParams` / `FlightGains` with `ContentSet`'s `FlightCommonDef`; `test` (AI-20, AI-06 follow-ups): the kits already pass it; `net`: none.
