Current web build S-FLIGHT-1

Play the flight spike (2026-10-13). A Dart on a flat-shaded 6 km moon: launch from the pad, fly, shoot targets, drop bombs, land tail-down.

  • Click the canvas to capture the mouse. W thrust, S brake, mouse aim, A/D roll, Q/E yaw, Shift boost, Space flip.
  • Left mouse or Ctrl: gun. Right mouse or V: bomb. Hold F: auto-dock. F1: tuning panel and help. Backspace: respawn.
  • Gamepad and touch work too. Tester protocol: docs/spikes/TA-30/TESTER_PROTOCOL.md.
