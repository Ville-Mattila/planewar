# sim_ai/brains - MODULE.md

Contract for `godot/src/sim_ai/brains/**`: the per-class ship autopilots of 05 s6.2-6.4 (AI-13 Dart; AI-14 Lancer,
Bomber, Scout) on one think / act pipeline (`DartBrain`) with the shared sub-machines, and the tower brain of 05 s6.8 (AI-17, `TowerBrain`). Sub-module of `sim_ai` (R0 B.9
A-21 split pattern, as `sim_ai/perception`): `sim_ai/MODULE.md` keeps `AiBrain`, `AiState`, `AiDriver`, `AiTag` and the
autopilot helpers the brains call (`Guidance`, `TeamPicture`, `LandingPilot`, `EscortPilot`, `Separation`,
`TargetChooser`). A change to "Public API" or "Invariants" is a contract change: contract tests first, same PR (ADR-11);
`tools/gen_module_api.py --check` must pass; <= 300 lines (ADR-14).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Responsibility | Dart, Lancer, Bomber and Scout brains: modes (the replicated `AiTag.Tag`), order dispatch, PULL_UP, the RTB rule, AttackRun (bombs, rockets), air combat, the Scout's search and flee. AI-17: `TowerBrain` (every tower def: target choice in the `towers.json` envelope, turret slew, trigger, the Missile Tower's lock hold). AI-16: `HarvesterBrain` (the mining cycle's autopilot, FLEE, route health) over the ground follower of `sim_ai`. AI-18: `CrawlerBrain` (the Siege Crawler: AUTO = HOLD, MOVE, ATTACK from a firing spot 1,000-1,150 m out, relocation after a near bomb, NO_AMMO hold) with `CrawlerParams` and `SiegeAim` (the lofted solve of R0 B.25 A-74). Not: the Hauler brain (AI-15), the Commander. |
| Lane owner tier | Opus (sim_ai lane) |
| May depend on | `core`, `content`, `sim` (`SimView`, `CommandSink`, steer); the rest of `sim_ai` |
| Must never touch | Nodes, `net`, `presentation`, `ui`; no `await`, signals, `Thread`, autoloads; never a `SimView` read of an entity the team does not see (05 s6) |
| Autoloads used | None |
| Status | AI-13 (2026-10-21): Dart. AI-14: Lancer, Bomber, Scout; AiState FORMAT 6 (`lock_tick`); `AiBrain.bind_intel`. AI-17: `TowerBrain`, `TowerParams`; AiState FORMAT 7 (`AiHitLog`). AI-46: DartCombat aim / merge statics (C-08). AI-16: `HarvesterBrain`, `HarvesterParams`, `HarvestSites`, `HarvestPicker.pick_sticky`. AI-18: `CrawlerBrain`, `CrawlerParams`, `SiegeAim`; `ap_crawler.follow` required (R0 B.29 A-145). AI-18b: the barrel (CR6). Constants: `ap_harvester` in `godot/content/defs/autopilots.json` (AI-47, R0 B.29 A-144); fixtures `ai13_autopilots.json`, `ai14_autopilots.json`, `ai17_autopilots.json` until AI-19 (needs content). |
